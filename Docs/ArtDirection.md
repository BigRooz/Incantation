# Art Direction

## Current Status

Visual and ambience work exists in the v0.1 prototype, but new art work is paused unless explicitly requested.

Do not modify assets, visuals, lighting, materials, models, prefabs, or scene dressing unless explicitly asked.

## Current Prototype Atmosphere

The current playable prototype includes:

- Dark cabin ambience.
- Ambient audio.
- Fire flicker.
- Hourglass light possession effect.
- Room veil.
- Seated table ritual staging.
- One real cursed book as the visual focus.
- Realtime visual word absorption.
- Wrong word rejection feedback.

## Current Guidance

Incantation should feel like a dark fantasy horror ritual, but the game is a competitive social party game first.

Visual work should support the seated table, the single cursed book, the hourglass, the visible phrase, and readable player reactions.

The room should reinforce pressure and atmosphere without distracting from the book, the table, the voice interaction, or the social chaos.

Do not add visuals that imply characters walk around during the ritual.

Do not add additional gameplay books.
