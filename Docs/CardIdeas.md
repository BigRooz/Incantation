## Current Status

Cards are now an explicitly requested data-and-presentation track.

The current foundation includes a three-card physical Spell Hand and `SpellDefinition` ScriptableObject presentation data. It does not include drawing, inventory, ownership, random selection, voice activation, spell execution, rarity probability, replacement, match integration, or networking.

Spell effects and interference gameplay remain paused unless a focused task explicitly activates them. Spell incantations remain separate from the core ritual word vocabulary.
